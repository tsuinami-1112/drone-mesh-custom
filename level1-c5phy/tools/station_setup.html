Level 1 station setup

Pick where a station stands, which way its face N points and what patches it carries. Save writes it to stations.ini as its own PlatformIO environment; flash it from there.

Antenna pattern (optional)

Leave empty and the firmware derives the beamwidth from the gain (8 dBi = 72°, 6 dBi = 90°) and the bearing constant K from the beamwidth. Fill in the datasheet beamwidth, or K from a stage 6 pattern sweep.

  1. Station position: click the map (drag the marker to adjust), paste, or use your location.
  2. Face N: switch to Face N direction and click a point the N patch faces, e.g. along a road or roof edge on the satellite layer. Or type the heading; a compass reads magnetic north, so add your local declination.
  3. Antennas: the patches' gain from their datasheet (the usual 5.8 GHz patch is 8 dBi). Dual-band patches on both bands make the station a dual-band build that sweeps 2.4 GHz too.
  4. Save, then flash the station from its own entry in PROJECT TASKS.
The map could not load (no internet?). Type or paste the coordinates and the heading instead; everything else works the same.
