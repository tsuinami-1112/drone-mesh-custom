Before you start: nothing in this station has run on hardware yet. The receiver is the C5's Wi-Fi PHY driven through undocumented calls that the C5VRX project proved on ESP-IDF 6.0; this firmware re-implements them on the fleet's Arduino/ESP-IDF 5.5 toolchain. The procedure on sheets 6–8 is ordered so that each stage proves the next one is worth doing: receiver alive, channel coverage, selectivity, video (NTSC then PAL), sensitivity against an RX5808 station, then the switch and the pattern. Two rules that are easy to break: the six I/Q lane pads (D0, D1, D2, D3, D6, D10) must stay unconnected, and every flash starts with a full erase. Full BOM on sheet 5.

1 · Full block diagram

Four patches feed an SP4T switch whose common port goes into the XIAO's U.FL antenna connector, directly or through the optional 20 dB LNA + band-pass module. Inside the C5 the Wi-Fi PHY is held receive-only on the FPV frequency; its raw I/Q leaves on the modem's diagnostic bus, travels out through six top-side GPIO pads (bits 9–7 of I and Q) and back into the PARLIO peripheral, and the firmware measures power, FM coherence and horizontal sync on 16 KiB windows. The XIAO's 3V3 regulator feeds the switch (and the LNA, or the 5 V rail does); the 5 V rail feeds the XIAO and the Heltec. The LNA sits after the switch, so all four sectors share one gain: no per-sector gain mismatch, and the bearing constant K is unaffected.

Each MODEM_DIAG bit is driven out through its pad and read back from it, so a load on a lane pad corrupts the samples.

2 · Sector layout and receiver

Top view. Each patch covers about 70°, so an emitter always sits inside one sector and near the edge of a neighbour. Unchanged from v2.

Method

Amplitude comparison. Take the strongest sector k and its two neighbours; with powers in dB the bearing is

bearing = az[k] + K · (P[k+1] − P[k−1])

K, in degrees per dB, is one constant (BEARING_K_DEG_PER_DB) taken from the stage 6 sweep of a VTX around the box in 15° steps. Report bearing_deg and bearing_sigma_deg; sigma grows when the strongest sector is within 3 dB of a neighbour (the emitter sits on a sector boundary), when the peak is weak, and when a neighbour's capture failed (then the axis of the strongest sector is reported with a wide sigma).

Per-channel scan loop

rf_tune(channel); delay(8 ms)
for sector in N,E,S,W:
    select(sector); wait 200 µs
    gain = 62
    for w in 1..3 (1 on a quiet channel):
        capture 16 KiB @ 40 MS/s     (0.4 ms)
        metrics                       (~2 ms)
        if clip > 3 %: gain -= 6; retake
    level = min(windows); q = median
hit = level[best] >= 8 dB and q[best] >= 40 %

Sweep ≈ 40 × (10 + 4 × 8 ms) ≈ 1.7 s at the full window count. The gain steps keep strong carriers unclipped and weak ones at full gain; the minimum over windows drops Wi-Fi bursts.

3 · Video check and serial output

Video check

On the strongest due peaks after the sweep: hold the channel and the sector, demodulate 8 windows 5 ms apart (phase step between samples = instantaneous frequency), find sync-tip pulses 3.2–7.2 µs wide below a threshold set from the window's own percentiles, and measure the spacing of consecutive pulses.

Line periodLine rateVerdictMeaning
64.0 µs (2560 samples)15 625 HzPALreal analog video carrier
63.56 µs (2542 samples)15 734 HzNTSCreal analog video carrier
sync-shaped pulses, no repeating period—nonenot video-shaped: noise, Wi-Fi, an unmodulated carrier
swing under 4 units/sample—noneno FM deviation on this channel

A window counts with ≥ 2 consecutive periods and a sync score ≥ 70 (width closeness 0–40 + period closeness 0–60); the carrier is video when ≥ 3 of 8 windows count. sync_q is the share of windows that counted. The field rate is not measured (a 410 µs window never sees a vertical interval): field_hz is the nominal 50/60 of the detected standard. The fingerprint fp is <video>/<sync_hz>/<freq_peak>, e.g. NTSC/15736/5734, where freq_peak is the tuned frequency plus the measured carrier offset — no synthesizer sweep needed.

Expected serial output

Boot line on USB at 115200. "rf":true is the PHY receive-only and the PARLIO reader running; false is followed by an {"info":"error",…} line naming the failed call. The bench console (? status, h R3 / h 5732 hold, s 0–3 sector, g 30 / g a gain, v video check, t 100 drive the switch lines while holding, b 0|1 analog filter BW20/BW40, x resume) prints {"info":"bench",…} lines with level_db, rssi_dbm, gain, p_mean, q_phase, clip, cfo_khz, mod, noise, step_std, nf_dbm.

5 · Bench procedure — is the receiver alive?

Work top to bottom on a bare XIAO with the stock antenna on the U.FL; the switch and patches come in stage 6. Do not move to the next stage until the current one gives the stated result. Console commands are typed into the serial monitor and Enter-terminated: ? status, h R3 / h 5732 hold, s 0–3 sector, g 30 / g a gain, v video check, t 100 drive the switch lines while holding, b 0|1 analog filter BW20/BW40, x resume.

Stage 0 — flash and boot

  • pio run -e seeed_xiao_esp32c5 --target erase, then --target upload. A full erase precedes every flash: stale PHY calibration data left C5VRX's receiver deaf after reflashes. Boot mode if it will not connect: hold BOOT, tap RESET, release BOOT.
  • Monitor at 115200. Expected: {"info":"c5phy v3 station ready",…,"rf":true}. If "rf":false, copy the {"info":"error"} line here:
  • ? twice, 60 s apart. Expected: captures climbing (hundreds per second while scanning), cap_err 0. Measured: captures/min cap_err

Stage 1 — receiver alive

  • No VTX on. h A1. Expected: level_db 0 ± 2, p_mean ≈ 2, q_phase under 15, gain 62, clip 0, noise 1, stuck 0 (a dead I/Q bus shows every sample identical: stuck 1 and bus_stuck climbing in the heartbeat; stuck at all zeros it also reads p_mean exactly 2.0, so trust the flag, not the number). Measured: level_db p_mean q_phase
  • Note the station's own no-signal noise floor: nf_dbm dBm. p_mean far from 2 means RF_NOISE_POWER is not this board's value — write it down for the calibration.
  • Key a VTX on A1 (5865 MHz) a few metres away. Expected: level_db up by ≥ 30 dB, gain stepping below 62, q_phase ≥ 50, mod 1, noise 0, cfo_khz within ±2000. Measured: level_db gain q_phase cfo
  • Move the VTX away or unkey it. Expected: the readings return to the no-signal values on the next line. level_db rising without q_phase rising means power but no FM coherence: an off-channel carrier (check cfo_khz) or scrambled lane order.

Stage 2 — channel coverage

  • x to resume scanning; wait for the first heartbeat (60 s). Expected: tune_fail 0, channels 40. Measured: tune_fail
  • If not 0: h each band's channels and note the ones refused ("tune refused by regulatory table"): . Set RF_COUNTRY_CC to the deployment country, reflash (erase first), repeat. Value used:
  • h E4 (5645, lowest) and h E8 (5945, highest). Expected: both hold; ? shows the bootstrap wifi_ch 132 and 177. Reading the numbers. level_db is dB above the noise reference (mean I²+Q² of 2.0 at gain 62); rssi_dbm = RSSI_CAL_DBM_AT_NOISE + level_db × RSSI_CAL_SLOPE + RSSI_CAL_OFFSET_DB − RF_FRONTEND_GAIN_DB. 7 · Bench procedure — switch, pattern, field, power

Stage 6 — RF switch and pattern

  • Eval board powered from 3V3, one patch on RF1, common port → XIAO U.FL, control lines from D7/D8/D9 through 2 kΩ. Hold a channel with a VTX on, drive each combination (t 000, t 100, t 010, t 110; the pattern stays until s or x) and record which RF port goes through. Write the truth table here (the firmware's V1 is D8, V2 D9, V3 D7): V1V2V3 = 000 → RF__    100 → RF__    010 → RF__    110 → RF__  → SECTOR_SWITCH_TABLE
  • VTX on, hold its channel, s 0 … s 3. Expected: level_db follows the sector the patch is on and settles within one line. Insertion loss against the patch wired direct to the U.FL: dB (expect ≈ 2); measured with the LNA in the path if fitted: dB (expect ≈ −18, a net gain)
  • Mount all four patches on the box, VTX at 30 m on a known bearing, clear line of sight. Rotate the box in 15° steps through a full turn, logging all four sectors values at each step.
  • Derive K in degrees per dB from the neighbour difference. Value: °/dB (default 3.0). Save the sweep to docs/ as the station's bearing calibration.

Stage 7 — field acceptance

  • Two v3 stations 300–500 m apart, both positions and face-N headings set in the mapper's LEVEL 1 STATIONS panel, both named to match their Meshtastic nodes, D4/D5 wired to the Heltecs.
  • Walk a VTX between them on ten marked points. Pass: the 90 % circle contains the true position on at least 9 of 10. Score: / 10
  • Pass: video reads NTSC or PAL on every report for a live camera, never none; the mesh copy arrives with fp.
  • Leave both stations on solar for 72 h. Pass: no brownout, heartbeat gap never exceeds 5 min. Measured station current at 5 V: mA (expect ≈ 200–240; ≈ 270–320 with the LNA, and peaks near 0.9 A while the Heltec transmits)
