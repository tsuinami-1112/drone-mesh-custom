Level 1 station setup

Pick where a station stands and which way its face N points. Save writes it to stations.ini as its own PlatformIO environment; flash it from there.

  1. Station position: click the map (drag the marker to adjust), paste, or use your location.
  2. Face N: switch to Face N direction and click a point the N patch faces, e.g. along a road or roof edge on the satellite layer. Or type the heading; a compass reads magnetic north, so add your local declination.
  3. Save, then flash the station from its own entry in PROJECT TASKS.
The map could not load (no internet?). Type or paste the coordinates and the heading instead; everything else works the same.
